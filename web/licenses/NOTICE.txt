SOURCE CODE
-----------
The complete source for these binaries, including every modification, is
available free of charge at

    https://github.com/jbn/simphp

The PHP 4.1.1 sources are unmodified except for the patches in patches/,
which are distributed separately from the original source as the Q Public
License requires. build.sh downloads php-4.1.1.tar.gz and rebuilds the
binaries from those sources, the patches and src/.
